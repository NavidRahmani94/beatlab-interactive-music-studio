# Demo script (4–5 minutes)
1. **Intro (20 s)** – "BeatLab: build, mix, save and export music in the browser."
2. **Drums (40 s)** – PLAY, edit steps live, switch kit to 808, mute the hi-hat, change BPM.
3. **Melody – Easy (60 s)** – pick a mood (Dreamy), show that every row is in key, add notes one by one, press ✦ AUTO MELODY, switch the Scale to Minor and say "the music changes with the scale".
4. **Melody – Advanced (40 s)** – open the piano roll, add a chord, drag the velocity lane to shape the phrase.
5. **Mixer (50 s)** – solo the melody, pan the hi-hat, add Master Reverb (Cathedral), show the level meter.
6. **Save / Open (30 s)** – Save, NEW, Open; open a broken file to show the error message.
7. **Export (30 s)** – export WAV, play it in another player. Mention: offline render, 44.1 kHz stereo 16-bit, same audio graph as live.
8. **Close (20 s)** – architecture slide (see ARCHITECTURE.md), limits (one bar, synthesised sounds), next steps (more bars, chords, sample instruments).
