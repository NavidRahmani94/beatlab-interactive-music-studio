# Demo script (5–6 minutes)
1. **Intro (20 s)** – "BeatLab: four tracks, a mixer, a master and a playlist – all in the browser."
2. **Drums (40 s)** – DRUMS tab: PLAY, edit steps live, switch kit to 808, mute the hi-hat, change BPM.
3. **Melody (60 s)** – MELODY tab: pick a mood (Dreamy), every row is in key; drag across an empty row to draw a long note, drag its end to stretch it; ✦ AUTO MELODY; switch Scale to Minor.
4. **Ambient + Arpeggio (60 s)** – AMBIENT tab: load SHIMMER (slow pad). ARPEGGIO tab: load MINOR CHORD, switch Up → Up-Down → Random, 1/8 speed, 2 octaves.
5. **Playlist (60 s)** – create a second pattern, place them on the bars, press SONG, PLAY; point at the highlighted bar and the visualiser. (Or load the sample “Full Song”.)
6. **Mixer + Master (50 s)** – 7 channels, solo the arp, pan the hi-hat; Master EQ, Compressor, Reverb (Cathedral), level meter.
7. **Save / Open (20 s)** – Save, NEW, Open; open a broken file to show the error message.
8. **Export (30 s)** – export the whole song as WAV and play it in another player. Mention: offline render, 44.1 kHz stereo 16-bit, same audio graph as live.
9. **Close (20 s)** – architecture slide (ARCHITECTURE.md), limits (patterns are one bar, synthesised sounds), next steps (longer patterns, chord helper, sample instruments).
